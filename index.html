About

Welcome to my website! I am a linguist with a focus on language documentation and description, typology, and morphosyntax. I recently completed my Research MA at Leiden University, where I graduated summa cum laude. I am now pursuing PhD opportunities and am excited to continue working on underdescribed languages.
